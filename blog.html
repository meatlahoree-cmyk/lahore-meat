Mutton

Mutton Lahore: Sahi Cut Kaise Chunein?

Har dish ke liye alag cut behtar hota hai. Mutton raan roast ke liye, chaamp grill aur karahi ke liye, qeema kabab aur kofta ke liye, aur dasti salan ke liye mashhoor hai.

Lahore Meat se fresh mutton Lahore mein Rs. 3200 per kg ke hisaab se har cut mil jata hai.

Beef

Beef Lahore: With Bone vs Boneless

Beef with bone salan aur nihari mein zyada taste deta hai, jabke boneless beef steak, bun kabab aur stir-fry ke liye behtar hai.

Hamare yahan beef with bone Rs. 1500 aur boneless Rs. 1850 per kg hai.

Frozen Chicken

Frozen Chicken Nuggets aur Patty Kaise Fry Karein

Frozen chicken ko fry karne se pehle fridge se nikal kar 5 minute rakhein, phir medium garam oil mein golden hone tak fry karein. Air fryer mein bhi tayyar ho jate hain.

Lahore Meat ke nuggets aur burger patty bachon aur mehmanon ke liye aasaan option hain.

Aqiqah Bakra Lahore: Zaroori Baatein

Aqiqah bachay ki paidaish par ki jane wali sunnat hai. Bakra sehatmand aur munasib weight ka hona chahiye. Hamare aqiqah bakra 12 se 16 kg tak available hain.
